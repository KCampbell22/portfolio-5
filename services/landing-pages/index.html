Services · Small businesses Landing pages for small businesses

A landing page has one job: get the visitor to call, book or buy. I build custom, fast-loading pages that look right on phones and make that next step obvious. I'm a freelance web developer in Grimes, near Des Moines, Iowa, working with businesses locally and remotely.

What's included

What you get

  • A custom page built for your offer, not a stretched template
  • Mobile-first layout, since most visitors arrive on a phone
  • Fast loading, tuned for Core Web Vitals
  • Clear calls to action: contact form, booking link or checkout
  • Search basics: page titles, descriptions and link previews
  • Accessible to every visitor, with WCAG in mind

Good fit for

  • Launching a new product, service or location
  • Running ads that need a page built to convert
  • Replacing an outdated one-page site
  • Agencies that need landing pages built for their clients

How it works

  1. Talk it through

    A short call about the offer, the audience and the action you want visitors to take.

  2. Plan the page

    We agree on sections, content and any design you already have, or I work from your brand.

  3. Build

    I build the page and share a preview link so you can try it on your own phone.

  4. Launch

    I connect forms or booking tools and get it live on your domain.

Questions

Do you work with businesses outside Des Moines?

Yes. I'm based in Grimes, near Des Moines, and happy to meet locally, but most of the work happens remotely, so location doesn't matter.

I don't have a design. Can you still help?

Yes. I can lay out the page from your logo, colors and content, or build from a design if you already have one.

Can you connect it to the tools I already use?

Usually, yes: contact forms, booking tools like Calendly, payment and email sign-up services.

Will I be able to update it?

We'll talk about how often you expect to change it, and I'll set it up so updates are straightforward.
